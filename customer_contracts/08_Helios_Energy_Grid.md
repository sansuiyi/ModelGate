# Helios Energy — AI Grid Analytics & Fault Diagnosis Agreement
## Contract ID: HE-2026-9913
## Effective Date: March 15, 2026

## PARTIES
This agreement is entered between Assurant, Inc. ("Provider") and Helios Energy
("Customer") for AI-powered grid analytics and predictive maintenance services.

## SECTION 1 — SERVICE DESCRIPTION
Provider shall deploy an AI analytics platform supporting:
- Smart grid sensor data anomaly detection
- Predictive maintenance scheduling
- Power load forecasting
- Fault diagnosis and root cause analysis

## SECTION 2 — DATA HANDLING AND PRIVACY
2.1 Grid operational data is classified as high privacy tier (critical infrastructure).
2.2 All data must be processed within EU data centers only (GDPR compliant).
2.3 No operational data may be transmitted outside the EU.

## SECTION 3 — APPROVED AI PROVIDERS
3.1 Approved providers: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT).
3.2 Google (Gemini) is not permitted due to data residency concerns.
3.3 Any China-based provider is strictly prohibited.

## SECTION 4 — PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Anomaly detection alerts within 2000ms.
4.2 Fault diagnosis (complex) may take up to 10000ms — accuracy prioritized (high_quality).
4.3 System uptime SLA: 99.99%.

## SECTION 5 — COST AND BILLING
5.1 Cost sensitivity is low; diagnostic accuracy is the primary objective.
5.2 Monthly budget cap: $30,000.

## SECTION 6 — USE CASE
6.1 Primary use case: grid analytics and fault diagnosis
6.2 Expected volume: 5,000 queries per day
6.3 Distribution: simple 30%, medium 40%, complex 30%
