# Vertex Retail Group — AI Personalized Shopping Assistant Agreement
## Contract ID: VR-2026-8823
## Effective Date: May 1, 2026

## PARTIES
This agreement is entered between Assurant, Inc. ("Provider") and Vertex Retail Group
("Customer") for AI-powered personalized shopping assistance services.

## SECTION 1 — SERVICE DESCRIPTION
Provider shall deploy an AI assistant supporting:
- Personalized product recommendations
- Order status and return handling
- Size and fit guidance
- Inventory and store availability queries

## SECTION 2 — DATA HANDLING AND PRIVACY
2.1 Customer shopping data is classified as standard privacy tier.
2.2 Data may be processed in US data centers only.
2.3 No payment card data shall be processed by the AI assistant.

## SECTION 3 — APPROVED AI PROVIDERS
3.1 Approved providers: OpenAI (GPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini).
3.2 China-based providers are strictly prohibited.

## SECTION 4 — PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Recommendations must return within 1200ms (low_latency).
4.2 Order status queries within 800ms.
4.3 System uptime SLA: 99.9%.

## SECTION 5 — COST AND BILLING
5.1 Cost sensitivity is medium; balance cost and personalization quality.
5.2 Monthly budget cap: $10,000.

## SECTION 6 — USE CASE
6.1 Primary use case: personalized shopping assistant
6.2 Expected volume: 25,000 queries per day
6.3 Distribution: simple 55%, medium 35%, complex 10%
